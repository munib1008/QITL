# ProphetCard

Entry point to a prophet profile. **No depictions**: the visual is the eight-point star, never a figure, face or silhouette.

- Provide `name`, `arabicName`, optional `honorific` (default "(عليه السلام)"; use ﷺ for Prophet Muhammad), `order` (relative order only when sourced), `mentions`, `href`.
- Whole card is the link; focus ring on the card.
