# HomePage

The finalised home-page design: navigation, hero, the JourneyTimeline, the feature cards and the footer, all on the `journey` theme. This is the reference Claude Code reproduces in Next.js. It is a showcase page, not a bundle export.

- Hero: the Basmala in `quran-inline` gold (`glow-gold`), an emerald kicker, the headline "From the beginning. / To the final Messenger." in `display-xl` (Source Serif 4) with the second line in gold (a solid colour, not gradient text), the lead paragraph, "Begin the journey" (primary) and "How it works", and a row of three facts.
- The three facts must stay true statements; no roadmap or vanity numbers.
- Navigation: the "Z" monogram in an emerald outlined square is an interim mark until a real logo exists. Links: The Journey, Explore, Methodology. A language toggle (EN · ع) and "Begin exploring".
- Feature cards stay labelled "Coming soon" until the atlas, evidence library and genealogy are actually wired.
- The footer repeats the principles: respecting evidence, preserving uncertainty, learning together.
