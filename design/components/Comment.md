# Comment

A threaded discussion unit attached to a source or story segment. Reading is public; posting, replying and reporting require an account.

- Provide `author`, `time`, `body`, optional `citation` (cite-a-verse), `role`, `edited`, `status` (`visible | hidden | removed`), `onReport`.
- Hidden/removed comments keep their place in the thread with a neutral explanation — never silently disappear.
- Comments are never presented as verified knowledge.
