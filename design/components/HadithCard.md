# HadithCard

A narration from one of the seven catalogued collections (Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i, Ibn Majah, Muwatta Malik).

- Provide `collection`, `number` (canonical), `numbering` (the numbering system used), `arabic`, `translation`, and `gradings` (array of HadithGrade props — every recorded grading, disagreements included).
- Arabic in `naskh-text`; translation in `body`.
- Zero gradings renders "Grade not recorded" — that is a valid, honest state.
