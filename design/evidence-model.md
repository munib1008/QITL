# Evidence model

How ZIQ shows the strength of what it says. Apply to every claim, date, place and relationship.

## The six evidence kinds

| Kind | Use when | Never |
|---|---|---|
| `quran` | The claim is the plain statement of a cited ayah. | For tafsir about the ayah — that is `scholar`. |
| `hadith` | A cited narration graded authentic (sahih/hasan) by a named scholar for that narration. | Because it sits in a collection. Sunan and Muwatta narrations are graded one by one. |
| `scholar` | A recognised scholar's interpretation, tafsir or inference. | Unattributed. Name the scholar and work. |
| `estimate` | A historian's date, place or sequence outside revelation. | For sacred history before recorded chronology. |
| `disputed` | Recorded scholarly or historical positions conflict. | To hide one side — show every position with its source. |
| `unknown` | Revelation and reliable history are silent. | As an apology. "This is not known" is a valid answer. |

## Dates

- `exact` — dot. Only with a primary source (rare before the Prophetic era).
- `range` — solid bar. Sourced lower and upper bounds (CE and AH shown together).
- `estimated` — dashed, hatched bar. A historian's approximation, labelled `estimate`.
- `relative` — diamond on the **relative-order track**, visually separate from the BCE/CE/AH axis. Used for Adam and early prophets.
- `unknown` — dotted band across the track.
- The Last Hour: signs are ordered only where texts order them; no date, no countdown, no "years remaining".

## Places

Verified point → pin. Approximate region → hatched `map-region` polygon. Multiple sourced candidates → each shown, labelled `disputed`. Historical hypothesis → dotted `map-hypothesis` outline. Unknown → listed beside the map with a "?" ring, never placed. Future-sign geography: broad regions only where a text names one.

## Genealogy

Solid edge = sourced; double edge = disputed/competing lines; dotted edge = unknown link. Every edge opens its claim. No "last descendant" claims.

## Hadith gradings

Show every recorded grading (scholar, grade, source). If gradings differ, show "Graded differently by other scholars" and the disagreement panel. Never aggregate into one badge that erases a dissent.
