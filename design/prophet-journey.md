Rules for editors:

- No years before the Prophetic era unless a historian's estimate is sourced and labelled as one. Dawud, Sulayman, Zakariya, Yahya and Isa carry historians' estimates; Ibrahim's dates vary too widely to show.
- Age markers come only from the Quran or authentic hadith: Nuh's 950 years among his people (29:14), and the Prophet ﷺ at 40 for the first revelation and 63 at his passing.
- Six placements are marked disputed (Idris, Ayyub, Dhul-Kifl, Ilyas, Al-Yasa', Yunus) and keep that label until the review board settles a reading.
- Two hadith references are still incomplete: the Hijrah narration number in Sahih al-Bukhari, and confirmation of Sahih al-Bukhari 4466 (age at passing) against the pinned edition.
