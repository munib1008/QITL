# Event Universe: research coverage

Generated by `pnpm tsx scripts/event-coverage-report.ts` from `content/events/inventory.draft.json`, `content/events/narrative-ranges.json` and the imported Quran text (Tanzil Uthmani). The same figures are shown, from the database, on `/admin/events/coverage`.

## The model in one page

- **Tables** (migration `20261031002450_event_universe.sql`): `historical_events` gains `track` (prophets, people, communities, events, parables, seerah), `ordering_kind`, `relative_position`, `name_source`, `dating_method` and `merged_into`; new tables `event_verse_refs`, `event_identifications` (identification, name, ordering and dating claims, each with a cited claim and a standing), `event_connections`, `event_dating_reviews`, `event_redirects` and `event_universe_stats` (counts only). Nothing parallel to `historical_events` or `people`.
- **Ordering kinds**: `relative` (a position within the track, ordering only, needs a cited ordering claim), `disputed` (at least two cited views), `unassigned` ("Order not established"; for parables "no historical date assigned"), `dated_estimate` (seerah and major events only; a historians' estimate with a named publication, a dating method, class C evidence and the separate historical/scientific review). Only a dated estimate may store a year; a database constraint refuses years for every other kind, and the page shows none.
- **Names**: `name_source = later_tradition` marks a name that is not in the cited passage. It cannot be published without a cited `name` claim, and the page says "Named in later tradition, not in the Quranic text". A traditional identification is a separate, cited claim that cannot be a "Quran" claim and is shown with every view when scholars differ.
- **Publishing**: the existing event guard (approved claims and a scholarly review) plus verse references on approved claims, the ordering rules above, and, for identifications and connections, their own claim and review (review types `event_identification`, `event_connection`, registered in `review_entity_types` and `review_entity_authors` with the author stamp). Published rows are fixed until moved to correction. The public sees published rows only; the empty state shows counts.
- **Merging** (`merge_events`): the source is archived, its slug redirects to the target, and claims, verse references, identifications, connections and links move over.
- **Regenerating**: edit `content/events/inventory.draft.json` (and `tests/fixtures/event-anchors.json`), run `pnpm tsx scripts/gen-event-inventory.ts` and this script. A test fails if the migration and the file disagree.

## How the inventory was built

1. The imported Arabic text (`content/quran/quran.txt`) was read surah by surah, with a word search to find where each prophet, person, community and event is named or described. The surah list and general knowledge were used only to find candidates.
2. Every reference was then checked against the text itself: the range must exist, and it must contain a named anchor word for the item (a name or a distinctive term). Ranges whose anchor was missing were corrected or dropped. `tests/unit/events.test.ts` re-runs this check on every reference in the inventory and in the migration.
3. Passages whose event or people are given only by later sources were not given an item; they are listed below as "to research".
4. A last pass looked for runs of verses that open like a narrative ("and when", "he said", "then when") and are not covered. It found six runs (7:48-52, 25:4-7, 34:31-35, 37:51-56, 38:60-62, 40:47-53). All six are scenes of the Hereafter or statements of opposition, now listed under "reviewed, not counted"; the only narrative verse among them, 40:53 (a one-line mention of the guidance given to Musa), is not covered by an item.

What this does **not** prove: that the register is complete. "Narrative" is an editorial judgement. The percentage below is the share of the *register* (the inventory plus the passages still to research) that an inventory item covers.

All 159 items are **drafts**. Nothing is public, dated or ordered: every item is in the "Order not established" lane until a reviewer adds a cited ordering claim. No year, Hijri or CE, is stored for any item.

## Identification and naming flags

These items carry `name_source: later_tradition`: a name used for the item is not in the cited passage (the tests check that the name does not occur in the text where that can be checked). The tradition behind the name is to be added as a separate, cited, disputed claim (`event_identifications`); it is never part of the item.

- **Habil and Qabil, the two sons of Adam** (people): 5:27-31
- **Musa and the servant of Allah (named al-Khidr in later tradition)** (people): 18:60-82
- **The battle of Uhud** (events): 3:121-129, 3:139-175
- **The pledge under the tree and the treaty at Hudaybiyyah** (events): 48:18-26, 48:27
- **The expulsion of a party of the People of the Book from their homes (identified in later tradition as Banu al-Nadir)** (events): 59:2-17
- **The expedition against the Byzantines in the hardship (identified in later tradition as Tabuk)** (events): 9:38-59, 9:81-96, 9:117-118

Other cases that need an identification claim before they are published, because the passage does not state them:

- **Ibrahim's son in the vision of the sacrifice** (37:99-113): the passage says "a forbearing boy" and does not name him; which son it was is disputed among scholars. The item is titled without a name.
- **Dhul-Qarnayn** (18:83-98), **the People of the Cave** (18:9-26), **the servant with Musa** (18:60-82): who or when they were is not in the text.
- **The Queen of Saba** (27:22-44): the passage says "a woman ruling them"; the name Bilqis is later tradition and is not used.
- **Uhud, Hudaybiyyah, Tabuk, Banu al-Nadir, the Trench**: the passages describe the events but do not use these names.
- **Companions of the Town** (36:13-29) and **the two owners of gardens** (18:32-44) are introduced by the text itself as examples ("set forth for them a parable"), so they sit in the Parables track and are never dated.
