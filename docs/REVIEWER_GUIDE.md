# Scholarly reviewer guide (first steps)

Nothing is published as reviewed knowledge until a scholarly reviewer approves it. The 159 story drafts are already in the review studio as drafts
(`/admin/stories`); the same text can be read, with the cited ayat in Arabic next to each claim, in `docs/review-packs/<slug>.md` or at `/drafts/<slug>` (public preview, marked "review pending").

1. The owner gives you the `scholarly_reviewer` role (`/admin/users`). Sign in with the emailed link.
2. Open a story in `/admin/stories`. Read each chapter against its cited ayat (the pack lists them verbatim) using the 12-point checklist in `docs/STORY_DRAFTS.md`.
   Start with the wordings the drafters flagged in the pack's story-level uncertainties.
3. For each chapter: Approve (the review is recorded against the chapter's current version), Request changes (with a note), or Reject. Editing a chapter after approval makes a new version and the approval no longer counts.
4. A story can be published only when its chapters have approved reviews of their current version and every claim behind them is approved. The database refuses otherwise.
5. You cannot review text you wrote yourself.
6. Hadith are not in these drafts; they are added later as separate, individually graded entries.
